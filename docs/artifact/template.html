Departing from Ben Gurion.

The basics, with links to the official Israel Airports Authority pages. Checked Nov 18, 2026; details can change.

When to arrive
3 h

before international flights, per IAA. Domestic flights: 2 h, and no later than 1 h. FlyCheck never suggests arriving later than this.

About the data

Where the answers come from.

FlyCheck looks at past departures from TLV recorded on the airport's official flight board. It is not a live flight tracker, and the past does not guarantee what your flight will do.

How late did flights leave?

Minutes after the scheduled time, n = departures. The board's time is close to takeoff, not gate departure.
