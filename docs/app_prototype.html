Sources

  • Flight archive: IAA flight board snapshots archived by The Octopus (odata.org.il), CC BY. Logged on 4 days (Jan 17, Mar 17, Jun 26, Jul 3 2026).
  • Airline reliability: TLVFlights airline-level statistics, used with attribution.
  • Price index: Israel Central Bureau of Statistics, series 121390.
  • Live board (planned card): data.gov.il, Israel Airports Authority.

Limitations

  • The labelled sample is small (734 departures) and comes from days just before and after the airport closure of Feb 11 – May 15, 2026. It cannot show seasonality.
  • Larger sources have been requested (a year-long archive of the same board; EUROCONTROL research data). The model will be retrained if they arrive.
  • Congestion is a proxy built from scheduled departures. It is not a measurement of queues or waiting time.
  • Historical patterns are not a guarantee about any future flight.
